Privacy Policy

Drillzo: Dig Deep
Effective: 2 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how Azhar Bhatti (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use Drillzo (“the game”).

Summary

Drillzo does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email or other personal details. Your game progress stays on your device. The game offers optional rewarded videos provided by Google AdMob, and optional one-time purchases through Google Play. Players who make any purchase are never offered videos.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress in each world (the map you have dug, cash, upgrades, relics found and play statistics), your purchases, your chosen pod paint, and your sound and music settings in local files on your device. This information is not sent to us or to any server we operate. It stays on your device until you clear the game’s data or uninstall the game.

Optional rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

Drillzo never shows ads on its own. In a few places the game offers an optional button such as “Watch a video: free fill-up”. Only if you tap it, a short video ad is shown and you receive the bonus. These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s ads software collects and processes some information automatically, including:

  • your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and launch time;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This information is collected and used by Google, is encrypted in transit, and is not received by us. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent using Google’s consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the pause menu. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional purchases

Drillzo offers optional one-time purchases (extra worlds and cosmetic pod paints) through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment and links the purchase to your Google account; we do not receive your payment details. The game checks with Google Play which items you own, so they can be restored on a new device, and remembers the result locally so they keep working offline. Making any purchase also turns off all video offers.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share any information ourselves. Information processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. It uses internet access and the advertising ID for the optional videos, and the Google Play Billing permission for optional purchases.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it. Google keeps advertising and purchase data according to its own policies and your Google account settings. You can contact us with any privacy question or request.

Children’s privacy

Drillzo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its features change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
